# Release Notes — Lex-Cybernetica Version 3.0

**Tag:** `v3.0.0`  
**Freeze date:** 8 September 2026  
**Status:** Founder-Frozen Publication Edition

## Major changes from Version 2.0

- Reorganises the framework into 20 Parts and 100 operative Articles.
- Restores and incorporates the complete five-tier SII Version 1.0 architecture.
- Adds Personhood recognition procedure and interim Personhood claimant protection.
- Makes rights operational through burdens, evidence, forums, review and remedies.
- Adds provider continuity, insolvency-transition and anti-lock-in protections.
- Operationalises Neural Trespass, cognitive privacy and cognitive-evidence minimisation.
- Adds modular Genesis/parentage/developing-Person architecture.
- Adds fork, merge, restoration, dormancy, succession and third-party-reliance rules.
- Adds civic anti-amplification without permanent disenfranchisement.
- Adds Administrative Anchor plus issue-specific cross-border connecting factors.
- Adds Taxonomy Escape and H0–H5 / E0–E5 / R0–R6 uncertainty controls.
- Preserves Version 2.0 as public legislative history.

## Legal status

Lex-Cybernetica remains a proposed future-law framework and legislative guideline. It is not enacted law or legal advice.
