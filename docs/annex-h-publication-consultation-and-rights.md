# ANNEX H — PUBLICATION, CONSULTATION AND RIGHTS

## H.1 Consultation protocol

A challenge to Version 3.0 SHOULD identify:

- the Article or Annex challenged;
- the proposition challenged;
- source or evidence;
- technical, legal, scientific or ethical objection;
- proposed correction;
- whether the issue calls for amendment, deletion, deferral, clarification or a new scenario test.

No outside comment, AI review or scenario result becomes Canon automatically.
