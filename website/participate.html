What makes feedback useful?

  1. Identify the Article or Annex challenged.
  2. State the proposition or assumption you believe fails.
  3. Provide a source, authority, counterexample or technical constraint.
  4. Explain the legal, scientific, technical or ethical objection.
  5. Propose a correction where possible.
  6. State whether the issue suggests amendment, deletion, deferral, clarification or a new scenario test.
