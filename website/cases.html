Cases & Future Law

Future cases are stress tests for doctrine.

Lex-Cybernetica uses scenarios to test whether a legal rule survives difficult facts involving embodiment, continuity, replication, control, identity and synthetic genesis.

01

Construct the boundary case

Use a scenario where existing legal categories collide or fail.

02

Identify the legal variables

Separate personhood, SII, control, continuity, embodiment, genesis and evidence.

03

Apply provisional doctrine

Test outcomes against dignity, safety, autonomy, equality and institutional feasibility.

04

Record failure modes

Revise doctrine when a rule produces contradiction, domination, incoherence or unadministrable results.
