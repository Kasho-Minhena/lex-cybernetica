The V3 spine

Five principles the framework refuses to collapse.

Version 3.0 turns the principle-heavy consultation draft into a more operational architecture while preserving the distinctions that protect dignity, agency and continuity.

01

Personhood is separate from SII

Technical integration classification cannot decide legal Personhood by itself.

02

Creation does not create ownership

Creating or commissioning an entity does not make a Person property.

03

Identity follows the Person

Legal continuity is not tied permanently to one body, device or substrate.

04

Genesis is not automatic Personhood

A genesis event begins analysis; it does not predetermine legal status.

05

Classification is not human worth

Technical categories must not become proxies for dignity, rights or legal value.
