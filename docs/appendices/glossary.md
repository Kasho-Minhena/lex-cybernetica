# Glossary of Defined Terms

## Analog Fallback State (AFS)

A tested safe local operating state available when connectivity, authentication, vendor support, or payment infrastructure fails.

## Biological Person

A living human being, irrespective of augmentation.

## Biological Substrate

A person's organic cellular and neurological systems. The term does not imply purity, superiority, or a fixed condition at birth.

## Cognitive Telemetry

Data that records, infers, predicts, or reconstructs neural activity, memory, attention, emotion, intention, sensory experience, or cognitive state.

## Custodian

A person that stores, hosts, transmits, maintains, or controls infrastructure supporting a cybernetic integration or synthetic person without thereby owning that person.

## Cyber-Jurisprudence Division (CJD)

The fictional specialist judicial division responsible for adjudication under Lex-Cybernetica.

## Cyber-Substrate Regulatory Authority (CSRA)

The fictional independent regulator responsible for licensing, technical standards, incident reporting, inspections, continuity planning, and enforcement.

## Cybernetic Integration (CI)

Hardware, software, synthetic tissue, prosthetic systems, neural interfaces, or remote computational services functionally integrated with a person.

## eSapient

A non-biological system recognized as a synthetic legal person under Section 6.

## Essential Function

A capability necessary to preserve life, health, bodily movement, communication, memory continuity, or meaningful access to civic life.

## Material Control

The practical ability to initiate, prevent, redirect, or terminate relevant conduct.

## Neural Intrusion

Intentional access to or interference with a protected neural system without valid authorization or another lawful basis.

## Operator

A person exercising Material Control over a cybernetic integration, network, or synthetic system.

## Right to Analog

The right of a Biological Person with cybernetic integration to enter a safe local operating state without losing Essential Functions.

## Substrate Fork

A separately executing instance derived from a cognitive state where more than one instance may claim continuity from the same pre-fork identity.

## Substrate Integration Index (SII)

A five-tier technical-risk classification based on functional mediation, remote dependence, delegated authority, reversibility, and consequence of failure. It does not measure intelligence, moral worth, citizenship, or entitlement to rights.

## Uploaded Neural Matrix (UNM)

A computationally instantiated cognitive architecture derived substantially from a biological person's recorded neural state.
