Why this framework exists

Law needs categories that survive technological change.

Lex-Cybernetica separates concepts that are often collapsed together: personhood and SII, identity and embodiment, creation and ownership, measurement and downstream rights, biological and synthetic genesis.

01

Personhood & continuity

How legal identity persists across changed bodies, devices, substrates, restoration, copies and forks.

02

Autonomy & control

How Essential Functions, Material Control, cognitive rights and the Right to Analog constrain domination over future persons.

03

Genesis & succession

How birth, cloning, synthetic creation, replication and successor-person events require distinct legal analysis.
