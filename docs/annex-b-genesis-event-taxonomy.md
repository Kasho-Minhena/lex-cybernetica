# ANNEX B — GENESIS EVENT TAXONOMY

The taxonomy is descriptive and non-exhaustive.

- **GE-01 — Biological sexual reproduction:** new organism from biological gametes.
- **GE-02 — Assisted biological reproduction:** IVF, assisted conception or artificial gestation while Personhood and parentage remain separately determined.
- **GE-03 — Synthetic-gamete reproduction:** gametes or gamete-equivalents created or materially engineered in vitro.
- **GE-04 — Multi-contributor genetic Genesis:** genetic contribution from more than two contributors.
- **GE-05 — Clonal Genesis:** substantial genomic copying from one source; identity is not presumed from genomic sameness.
- **GE-06 — Engineered biological Genesis:** new biological Person or developing entity with material genome or epigenome engineering.
- **GE-07 — Bio-synthetic Genesis:** biological development materially dependent on synthetic organs, support systems or substrate.
- **GE-08 — Synthetic cognitive Genesis:** a newly created artificial-origin cognitive entity without a prior personal continuity claim.
- **GE-09 — Cognitive-derivative Genesis:** a new cognitive entity built partly from another Person's memories, architecture, preferences or model.
- **GE-10 — Fork Genesis:** one continuing cognitive state becomes two or more independently continuing successor candidates after divergence.
- **GE-11 — Replication Genesis:** a copy of a Person-state is instantiated while the source remains active; Personhood depends on continuity and independent persistence.
- **GE-12 — Collective / emergent Genesis:** a persistent candidate mind arises from several contributing minds or systems.
- **GE-13 — Self-Genesis:** a Person deliberately creates a distinct descendant or successor without another co-parent.
- **GE-14 — Restoration after discontinuity:** normally a continuity event rather than new Genesis; may become a successor-Person question where continuity cannot be established.
