# Part IX — Sex, Gender, Reproductive Configuration and Embodiment


## WD-48 — Biological sex characteristics

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Biological sex characteristics are biological/medical attributes where applicable. They are not a universal identifier, a measure of personhood, or an automatic determinant of gender, reproductive configuration or SII.


## WD-49 — Reproductive configuration

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Reproductive Configuration records functional reproductive/genesis capabilities where relevant, including gametic, gestational, synthetic-gamete, artificial-gestation, self-/code-genesis, non-reproductive or not-applicable states. It may change over time.


## WD-50 — Gender identity

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Gender identity is a legal/social identity of the Person and must not be inferred solely from sex characteristics, reproductive capability, prosthetics, synthetic organs, embodiment, appearance, voice, creator intent or SII.


## WD-51 — Embodiment presentation

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Embodiment presentation describes the current physical or virtual form through which a Person manifests. It is distinct from personhood, identity, biological sex characteristics, reproductive configuration and gender identity.


## WD-52 — Prosthetics and gender

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Acquisition, removal or change of genital, reproductive, endocrine, sensory or other prosthetics may alter embodiment, reproductive configuration and sometimes SII, but does not by itself determine or invalidate gender identity or Root Continuity Identity.


## WD-53 — Data minimisation for sex/gender/reproduction

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Identity, SII and public registries must not collect or disclose biological sex, gender, reproductive configuration or genesis-provenance data unless the specific lawful purpose requires it.
