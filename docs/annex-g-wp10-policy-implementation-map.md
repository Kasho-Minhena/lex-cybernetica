# ANNEX G — WP10 POLICY IMPLEMENTATION MAP

*Development provenance only; non-operative. These internal policy dispositions informed Version 3.0 drafting but are not independent legal authority.*

| Policy | Version 3.0 implementation |
|---|---|
| POL-01 Forked Marriage | Articles 72 and 81: successor Personhood independent of spouse consent; marriage does not automatically multiply; consent and fair allocation of accrued duties required. |
| POL-02 Synthetic Daughter | Articles 36–40 and 42–44: dependency-causing responsibility without ownership; provider/IP duties proportionate and separable. |
| POL-03 Five-Parent Child | Articles 60–65: modular parentage, non-waivable duties and accountable coordination. |
| POL-04 Restored Dead Identity | Article 76: rebuttable same-Person continuity presumption with preserved historical death/interruption event. |
| POL-05 Third-Party Reliance | Article 77: good-faith reliance protected; fraud/collusion exceptions; targeted restitution/compensation rather than indiscriminate clawback. |
| POL-06 Hundred Voters | Articles 83–85: current-cycle anti-amplification and prospective ordinary qualification. |
| POL-07 Three-Week Adult | Articles 11–15 and 64: an adult-capable successor retains the capacity presumption; short synthetic or successor age alone cannot create minority. Genuine developing Persons remain protected by applicable child/development law and capacity-sensitive safeguards. |
| POL-08 Borderless Person | Articles 86–90: one Administrative Anchor plus issue-specific substantive connectors. |
