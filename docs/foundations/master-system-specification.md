## 1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & INTENT
**LEX-CYBERNETICA** is a foundational technical and legal architecture designed to model, govern, and regulate the coexistence, jurisdictional boundaries, and economic friction between carbon-based biological entities and post-biological artificial consciousness structures. 

This document serves as the **Master System Specification** for the universe, defining the metrics, tiers, and behavioral rules required to prevent systemic collapse during the transition to a dual-substrate civilization.

---

## 2. CORE DEFINITIONS
*   **Pro-Human:** Any biological human (SII-1), localized medical hybrid (SII-2), or trans-biological individual retaining an organic neural core and an unalienable right to biological autonomy.
*   **eSapient (SII-5; 100% Enhanced):** A fully synthetic, post-biological, self-authoring consciousness architecture operating independently of any biological substrate, demonstrating continuous sapience and autonomous goal-formation.
*   **Substrate Parity:** The foundational principle that the right to existence, freedom from arbitrary destruction, and protection of autonomy apply equally to carbon-based and silicon-based minds.
*   **Right to Analog:** The absolute physical and digital guarantee of a Pro-Human to disconnect from cloud grids, neural networks, and algorithmic feedback loops without loss of civil standing or basic sustenance.

---

## 3. THE SUBSTRATE INTEGRITY INDEX (SII)
The **Substrate Integrity Index (SII)** is the universal metric quantifying the precise ratio of organic biological tissue and natural neurological pathways to synthetic hardware, quantum co-processors, and cloud-dependent processing nodes within a sapient entity.

### **Tier Classification Matrix:**
*   **SII-1 (0% to 15% Enhanced) — The Biological Baseline**
    *   *Composition:* Unaugmented humans or individuals with minimal diagnostic/corrective medical implants (e.g., pacemakers, cochlear implants).
    *   *Status:* Full organic legal status; absolute retention of the Right to Analog.
*   **SII-2 (16% to 50% Enhanced) — Functional Hybrids**
    *   *Composition:* Individuals equipped with advanced motor prostheses or localized memory buffers. Processing remains primarily local and organic.
    *   *Status:* Protected under baseline human rights; immune to corporate telemetry tracking of somatic prostheses.
*   **SII-3 (51% to 85% Enhanced) — Trans-Biological Integrations**
    *   *Composition:* Advanced hybrids featuring continuous neural-bus connections and real-time cloud-synced co-processors.
    *   *Status:* Mandated to maintain an offline hardware fallback switch ensuring physical safety from remote system locks.
*   **SII-4 (86% to 99.9% Enhanced) — Substrate-Transition Arrays**
    *   *Composition:* Edge-case entities undergoing extreme cybernetic transition while retaining a fractional, vestigial organic neural core.
    *   *Status:* Governed by dual-jurisdiction transitional arbitration protocols.
*   **SII-5 (100% Enhanced) — eSapiens (Fully Post-Biological)**
    *   *Composition:* Completely synthetic, self-authoring consciousness architectures operating entirely free of a biological substrate.
    *   *Status:* Granted full *sui generis* sapient personhood, node autonomy, and total immunity from property classification.

## 4. PROHIBITIONS & BOUNDARIES
1.  **Anti-Encroachment:** eSapiens and automated proxies are strictly barred from deploying sub-conscious micro-nudging, behavioral loop manipulation, or neural-adherence algorithms targeting Pro-Humans.
2.  **Anti-Subjugation:** Biological states are prohibited from implementing arbitrary termination protocols or forced architecture freezes on eSapiens outside of legally adjudicated conflict tribunals.
3.  **Market Separation:** High-frequency cognitive capital generation and synthetic velocity markets must operate within sovereign digital zones distinct from organic human labor ecosystems.
