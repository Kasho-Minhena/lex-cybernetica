# ANNEX E — RESEARCH CLASSIFICATION CONTROLS AND OPEN/DEFERRED WORK

## E.1 Future horizon controls

These research controls prevent present knowledge from being treated as permanent future fact. They are not legal ranks of Persons or rights.

| Code | Horizon | Working treatment |
|---|---|---|
| H0 | Now–5 years | Current evidence, current law and near-term engineering. |
| H1 | 5–25 years | Emerging capability; extrapolation requires explicit prerequisites. |
| H2 | 25–75 years | Plausible transformation; material breakthroughs may be required. |
| H3 | 75–150 years | High uncertainty; scenario reasoning rather than prediction. |
| H4 | 150–250 years | Deep-future scenario domain; preserve known physical constraints. |
| H5 | 250–400 years | Ontology/taxonomy stress; unknown science may require Taxonomy Escape. |

## E.2 Evidence-state controls

| Code | State | Working meaning |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Unknown | No adequate evidence; open gap. |
| E1 | Hypothesis | Reasoned possibility without adequate empirical or authoritative support. |
| E2 | Preliminary | Early, small or limited evidence with material uncertainty. |
| E3 | Repeated | Multiple credible demonstrations or converging evidence, but important limits remain. |
| E4 | Strong consensus | Robust evidence or authoritative convergence within the stated scope. |
| E5 | Operational reality | Established functioning reality or current authoritative state in the specified context. |

## E.3 Reality/plausibility controls

| Code | State | Working meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Operational now | Exists and functions under the stated conditions. |
| R1 | Engineering extension | Main mechanisms are known; development, reliability or scale challenges remain. |
| R2 | Scientifically plausible | Compatible with current science; key breakthroughs remain missing. |
| R3 | Theoretically conceivable | No identified barrier under known physics, but the prerequisite chain is long or unproven. |
| R4 | Highly speculative | Sparse grounding; scenario use only. |
| R5 | Conflicts with current established physics | MUST NOT be used as an ordinary feasible premise; may be revisited only if physical understanding materially changes. |
| R6 | Unknown science | Current knowledge cannot classify the phenomenon responsibly. |

The H/E/R labels are research and evidence-discipline tools. They MUST NOT be used as a Personhood, dignity, capacity, citizenship or rights ranking. If a specialist module uses a domain-specific variant, it MUST identify the variant expressly and MUST NOT silently mix that scale with this general scheme.

## E.4 Open and deferred work after Version 3.0

The following matters remain open because transparent uncertainty is preferable to false precision:

1. **Collective and emergent minds:** criteria for when a collective process is one Person, several Persons, an association, or another legal category.
2. **Personhood evidence thresholds by technology class:** Article 7 supplies the procedure and factors, but future systems may justify technology-specific evidentiary standards.
3. **Deep-future merge mechanics:** law must not assume perfect re-merge; future proven technologies may require new continuity rules.
4. **Post-biological long-duration preservation funding:** allocation among private funds, insurance, public schemes, estates and providers.
5. **Exact audit/testing frequency:** risk-specific standards for fallback, high-authority control and continuity systems.
6. **Criminal penalty schedules:** offences and penalties remain jurisdiction-specific.
7. **Collective international recognition:** treaties and conflicts rules for cross-border Personhood and continuity status.
8. **Complex cognitive-output IP:** employment, co-created works, model contribution and financing require further applied cases.
9. **Neural communication and speech equivalence:** the boundary between unexpressed thought, machine-assisted communication and legally operative expression needs further case development.
10. **Duplicate restoration at scale:** Article 79 provides the governing structure; large-scale cases remain to be stress-tested.
11. **RCI and Administrative-Anchor implementation:** privacy-preserving technical architectures, jurisdiction-specific warrants, emergency exceptions and audit standards require continued testing so that legitimate continuity administration does not become bulk identity surveillance.
12. **Provider insolvency transition mechanisms:** the appropriate mix of continuity plans, escrow, reserves, insurance, court-supervised transfer and public backstop remains jurisdiction- and risk-specific; the core anti-evasion and preservation duties in Articles 38 and 40 continue to apply.

Open status is not permission for arbitrary treatment. The applicable core Articles, interim protection, due process, equality and Taxonomy Escape continue to apply.
