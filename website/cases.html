Cases & Future Law

Future cases are stress tests for doctrine.

Lex-Cybernetica uses scenarios to test whether a legal rule survives difficult facts involving embodiment, continuity, replication, control, identity, dependence and synthetic genesis.

01

Construct the boundary case

Use a scenario where existing legal categories collide or fail.

02

Identify the variables

Separate Personhood, SII, control, continuity, embodiment, genesis, evidence and responsibility.

03

Apply V3 doctrine

Test outcomes against dignity, safety, autonomy, equality, evidence and institutional feasibility.

04

Record failure modes

Identify contradiction, domination, incoherence or unadministrable results without silently changing Canon.

Governance boundary

Scenario evidence is not Canon.

Published case analysis must distinguish facts, assumptions, doctrine applied, unresolved questions and alternative outcomes. No scenario is represented as a prediction of enacted law.
