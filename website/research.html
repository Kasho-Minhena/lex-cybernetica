Research

A framework built to expose hard questions, not hide them.

Lex-Cybernetica treats future law as a research problem spanning doctrine, philosophy, technology, medicine, governance and social institutions.

Research line 01

Personhood without substrate essentialism

What facts should matter when law recognises a Person whose embodiment, cognition or continuity may be distributed across biological and technical systems?

Research line 02

Continuity, forks and successor persons

When does restoration preserve identity, when does replication create another Person, and when does a new successor relationship arise?

Research line 03

Control without ownership of persons

How should legal systems distinguish ownership of components and intellectual property from impermissible ownership or control of a recognised Person?

Research line 04

Institutions for uncertain futures

Which courts, registries, standards, review bodies and evidentiary processes could make future-law decisions contestable and explainable?

Research method

Doctrine → challenge → scenario test → revision.

The public framework preserves deferred and rejected propositions as legislative history instead of silently rewriting the record. Scenario work tests doctrine against difficult boundary conditions.

  1. 1Frame the legal problem.
  2. 2Separate concepts that should not be mechanically linked.
  3. 3Draft provisional doctrine.
  4. 4Stress-test with cases, science and comparative law.
  5. 5Preserve dissent, revision and provenance.
