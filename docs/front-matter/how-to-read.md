# How to Read This Treatise

Lex-Cybernetica uses the architecture of a modern statutory treatise to present proposed and expected law for future sapiens.

## Order of authority within the proposed system

Where two provisions appear to conflict, read them in the following order:

1. the constitutional principles and fundamental rights in Volume I;
2. the operative statutory sections of the Core Statutory Code;
3. the procedural and evidentiary rules in Volume III;
4. the CSRA regulations and technical standards in Volume II; and
5. the illustrative judicial interpretations in Volume IV.

The Master System Specification supplies common terminology but does not override the more detailed statutory text.

## Editorial commentary

Passages marked **Commentary** explain the design logic behind a rule. They are persuasive explanations, not independent legal commands inside the proposed system.

## Defined terms

Capitalized terms receive their principal meanings in Section 2 of the Core Statutory Code. The glossary consolidates those definitions for reference. A definition should be read in context and together with any more specific rule governing the subject.

## Illustrative citations

The bracketed year and CJD report number used in Volume IV imitate a neutral case-reporter form. They do not refer to real courts or published authorities.

## Canonical status

The Markdown maintained in the canonical repository is the source edition. GitBook and ebook editions are publication formats derived from that source.
