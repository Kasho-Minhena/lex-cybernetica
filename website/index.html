It separates legal status from technical classification and builds operational rules for identity, autonomy, control, continuity, responsibility and cross-border recognition.

The V3 spine

Five principles the framework refuses to collapse.

Version 3.0 turns the principle-heavy consultation draft into a more operational legal architecture while preserving the core distinctions that protect dignity, agency and continuity.

01

Personhood is separate from SII

Technical integration classification cannot decide legal Personhood by itself.

02

Creation does not create ownership

Creating, commissioning or designing an entity does not make a Person property.

03

Identity follows the Person

Legal continuity is not tied permanently to one biological body, device or substrate.

04

Genesis is not automatic Personhood

A genesis event begins analysis; it does not predetermine legal status.

05

Classification is not human worth

Technical categories must not become proxies for dignity, rights or legal value.

Challenge the framework

“No outside comment, AI review or scenario result becomes Canon automatically.”

Version 3.0 is founder-frozen for this publication edition, but criticism remains valuable. Later substantive change requires a separately versioned amendment or successor edition.

A useful challenge should identify:

  1. the Article or Annex challenged;
  2. the proposition challenged;
  3. source or evidence;
  4. the technical, legal, scientific or ethical objection;
  5. the proposed correction;
  6. whether the issue calls for amendment, deletion, deferral, clarification or a new scenario test.

This structure follows Annex H of the Founder-Frozen Publication Edition.
