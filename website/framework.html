Framework

Eleven parts for a legal system that can reason about future persons.

The framework separates status, control, embodiment, identity, genesis and institutional questions so that one technical characteristic does not mechanically determine legal worth.

Governing interpretive principles

  1. Personhood is separate from SII.SII describes a configuration; it does not rank dignity, humanity, intelligence or legal worth.
  2. Substrate parity.Biological or technical difference may justify necessary safeguards, not lesser dignity.
  3. Identity follows the Person.Embodiment and substrate may change without automatically changing legal identity.
  4. Creation does not create ownership.A recognised Person cannot be owned merely because another actor created, financed, trained, gestated, hosted or designed components used in that Person's genesis.
