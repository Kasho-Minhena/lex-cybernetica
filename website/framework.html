Framework

Twenty Parts for a legal system that can reason about cybernetic integration and future Persons.

Version 3.0 separates Personhood, technical classification, control, identity, continuity, genesis, cognitive integrity, responsibility and institutional review so that no single technical characteristic determines legal worth.

Governing interpretive principles

  1. Personhood is separate from SII.SII describes a defined technical configuration; it does not rank dignity, intelligence, humanity or legal worth.
  2. Substrate parity.Biological or technical difference may justify necessary safeguards, not lesser dignity or status.
  3. Identity follows the Person.Embodiment, substrate and technical configuration may change without automatically changing legal identity.
  4. Creation does not create ownership.A recognised Person cannot be owned merely because another actor created, financed, trained, hosted or designed components involved in that Person's genesis.
  5. Classification is not legal worth.Technical categories are tools for risk, dependence and control analysis—not substitutes for Personhood.
