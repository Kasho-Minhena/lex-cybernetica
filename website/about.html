About

Lex-Cybernetica is a proposed future-law framework.

Its purpose is to develop legal categories and institutional safeguards for societies in which cybernetic integration, synthetic cognition, non-biological embodiment and new forms of genesis may challenge current law.

The project

A legislative architecture, not a technology product.

Lex-Cybernetica asks how law should recognise persons, allocate responsibility, protect autonomy and preserve equality when technical configuration no longer maps cleanly onto traditional biological assumptions.

The framework is deliberately provisional. External expert review, comparative-law research, scientific validation, scenario testing and future ratification remain part of the development process.

What it is / is not

Clear public boundaries.

Lex-Cybernetica is

  • A future-law research framework
  • A consultation draft
  • A structured body of provisional doctrine
  • A platform for external criticism and refinement

Lex-Cybernetica is not

  • Enacted law
  • Legal or medical advice
  • A certification standard
  • A claim that any jurisdiction has adopted the framework
